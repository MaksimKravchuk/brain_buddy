Provider artwork is bundled locally so the sign-in controls do not require a
third-party script, image request, or font request.

- `google.svg`: current gradient Google G, copied without modification from
  [SVGL](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/static/library/google.svg).
  Google branding remains Google's trademark. Button appearance follows
  [Google Identity branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).
- `apple.svg`: Apple Design Resources “Logo - SIWA - Left-aligned - White -
  Medium” artwork, with its original 31×44 artboard and glyph path, mirrored in
  [MySpeedPuzzling](https://github.com/MySpeedPuzzling/myspeedpuzzling.com/blob/4537a087023941aef01622caee384ff21ad6780b/templates/_social_provider_logo.html.twig).
  The black sample background is drawn by the button. Preserve the artwork's
  built-in vertical padding. Original source: [Apple Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/)
  (`Logo-Sign-in-with-Apple.dmg`).
  Apple branding remains Apple's trademark. Button appearance follows
  [Sign in with Apple guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple).
- `google-sans-latin-500-normal.woff2`: Google Sans Medium Latin subset from
  `@fontsource/google-sans` 5.3.1. Its SIL Open Font License is preserved in
  `GoogleSans-OFL.txt`.
