X-01 · Sidebar footer status line (macOS)

Replaces today's footer (account name plus the internaldrive icon "Stored on this Mac", or the account name plus "Sign out") with one short line of words (FR-012). The account email and "Sign out" move into the status popover (X-02). The line is quiet: secondary label colour, 11 pt, no colour-only signal, no glyph in calm states. A fixed 12 pt slot at the trailing edge holds the activity indicator, so the words never move when it appears (FR-013).

Two click targets at most. The status words open the popover (FR-016) in every state. A trailing action after " · " appears in two states only: "Sign in to sync" (opens the sign-in sheet, X-03) and "Retry" (runs Sync now). Example data: today Tue 6 Oct 2026, 14:34; the sidebar shows the 020 PR-06 "Weekly review · coming later" row, which lands first.

Rules the screens above rely on

  • Precedence when several states hold: session ended → changes couldn't sync → couldn't sync → offline → synced with changes waiting → synced. The most actionable state wins; the popover always shows all of them.
  • No flicker: the words change only when the state changes, never because a sync started or stopped. The indicator slot is always reserved.
  • Never interrupts: no state opens a sheet, alert, notification, sound or badge, and none moves focus or selection (FR-009, FR-013, FR-017, SC-004).
  • VoiceOver: the status words are one button, "Sync status: Synced 3 minutes ago. Show details". The trailing action is a separate button ("Sign in to sync", "Retry sync"). A state change into an attention state (session ended, couldn't sync, changes couldn't sync) is announced once at polite priority; calm changes (minutes ticking, syncing) are not announced. The indicator has the label "Syncing" and is not announced on appearance.
  • Offline while the session expired: nothing about the session shows until the Mac is online and the server refuses it; until then the line says "Offline · N changes waiting" (edge case).
