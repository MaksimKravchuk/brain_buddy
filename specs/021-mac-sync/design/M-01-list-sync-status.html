M-01 · List-screen sync status, aligned with the Mac (iOS)

The iPhone already syncs in the background and already shows sync in words (SyncStatusLabel) at the bottom of every list, under an empty state, in the Lists hub's Settings row and at the top of Settings › Sync. FR-019 aligns it with the Mac (X-01): the same states, the same wording with "iPhone" for "Mac", and the same compact form. Concretely:

(1) "Syncing…" text goes away: during a sync the words stay and a small indicator appears beside them. (2) A failure is no longer shown at once as "Sync failed — reason" with the reference ID under it; transient failures show only the indicator, and only after 60 s of failure does the line become "Couldn't sync · Retry". (3) The em dash becomes " · " and relative times become short ("3 min ago", not "3 minutes ago"). (4) The cloud glyphs go; a warning glyph appears only in attention states. (5) In attention states the row becomes one 44 pt button: Retry, Sign in again, or open Sync issues. Settings keeps its detailed Sync section and the Sync issues screen, with the new wording on its status line (FR-019). No dialog, no badge, no notification, ever (FR-017).

Behaviour, VoiceOver and touch

  • Same rules as the Mac: precedence (session ended → changes couldn't sync → couldn't sync → offline → waiting → synced), no text change during a sync, failures only after 60 s except an ended session, cleared by the next success, relative time refreshed at least once a minute.
  • Tap targets: attention rows are a single button at least 44 pt tall across the row width. Calm rows are not interactive.
  • VoiceOver: calm rows read as text ("Synced 3 minutes ago"). Attention rows are buttons with a hint ("Couldn't sync. Retry", hint "Tries to sync again"; "2 changes couldn't sync", hint "Opens sync issues"). Entering an attention state is announced once; minutes ticking and syncing are not.
  • Contrast: slate-500 on white for 13 pt secondary text is 4.8:1; amber-800 on white 7.1:1; sky-700 on white 5.9:1.
  • Reduce Motion: static glyph instead of the rotating indicator. Never: dialog, alert, badge, sound or notification for routine sync (FR-017, SC-004).
