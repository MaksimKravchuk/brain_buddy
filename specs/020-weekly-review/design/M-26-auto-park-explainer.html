M-26 · Auto-park explainer — one-time sheet at first app open (iOS)

New on 2026-10-06 (owner decision PD-3, FR-051). The first time the person opens the app after the feature is switched on for them, before anything else, a short sheet explains the threshold rule and auto-park. It is independent of the review onboarding (M-12). Dismissing it ("Got it" or Close) records that it was seen; that moment starts the 14-day grace for tasks already in Next (FR-016), and auto-park never runs before it. Seen once on any device means never shown again on the others. Example: first opened today, Fri 9 Oct, threshold 14 days.
