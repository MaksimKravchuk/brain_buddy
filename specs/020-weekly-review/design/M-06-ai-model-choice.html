M-06 · When Apple's on-device model is unavailable — choice and download (iOS)

Shown in place of the navigator panel after "Suggest" when Apple's model can't serve this task (FR-023): unsupported device, Apple Intelligence off, model not ready, or unsupported language. It says which reason applies, then offers (a) a separate downloadable on-device model, with its size shown before anything starts, if this iPhone can run it, and (b) the named cloud provider, which always goes through consent (M-07). "Not now" leaves the card fully usable. The choice is remembered and changeable in Settings (M-23).

Size "1.1 GB" and provider "OpenAI" are illustrative; the model and provider are planning decisions (spec Assumptions). Example task is in Russian, the clarified trigger case.
