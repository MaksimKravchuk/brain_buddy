M-01 · Next actions with age markers — iOS (Next tab)

Extends TaskListScreen for the Next list. Order and project grouping are unchanged; a marker is added to the row's metadata line. Only two states appear in the list: asks for a decision as an indigo chip and moves to Someday tomorrow as an amber chip. Fresh and ageing tasks show no marker here; "ageing" is shown only in task detail (M-02), by owner decision on 2026-10-05. Every marker is a word plus an icon, never colour alone. Rose stays reserved for real due dates. No marker ever says "late" or uses error styling (FR-004, FR-038).

Tapping "Asks for a decision" or "Moves to Someday tomorrow" opens the decision card (M-03) as a large-detent sheet over Next (decided). Example data: threshold 14 days, today Fri 9 Oct 2026.
