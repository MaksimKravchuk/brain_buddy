Review step chrome (shared by M-13 to M-22): "Leave" on the left, mode and step position in the middle, "Skip" on the right (FR-029: every step skippable). A segmented progress bar under it: filled = done, grey = skipped, dark = current. The step's own primary action sits in the bottom bar. Leaving keeps everything done so far; the review resumes at the same step on any device (US4-7).
