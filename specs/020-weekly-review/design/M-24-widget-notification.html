M-24 · Next actions widget with the "asks for a decision" count, M-25 · the weekly notification — iOS

M-24 extends NextActionsWidget (list = Next actions) with one indigo chip, "N ask", next to the existing count (FR-037). The chip appears only when N > 0; zero is not shown. The chip is tappable (owner decision 3). In medium and large it is a WidgetKit Link to brainbuddy://review/decisions, with a 44 × 44 pt hit area, and opens the review's decision step at its first card (M-16). If onboarding was never done, it opens onboarding (M-12) first. If a review is open, the link resumes that review at its decision step; otherwise it starts a quick review at the decision step, with Wins and Inbox marked skipped. Small widget: WidgetKit gives a small widget a single tap target, so the chip there can't be a separate link. To meet FR-037, the whole small widget opens the decision step while N > 0, and opens Next as today when N = 0. Its completion button still works in place. The owner must confirm this (see design.md). Rows, completion buttons, Inbox badge and capture link are unchanged. The widget never syncs; the count is computed from the shared store like the rest of the entry, and the timeline also refreshes at the next marker change (at least at midnight, as today). When the widget is configured for "Today", no chip is shown. VoiceOver: "3 tasks ask for a decision".

M-25: at most one local notification per week at the chosen day and time (FR-036). There is no notification if a complete or partial review happened in the preceding 6 days. No follow-ups, no counts, and no task titles on the lock screen. Tapping opens the review entry (M-11). The web sends no notifications.
