M-09 · While you were away — iOS

Lists auto-parked tasks the person has not seen yet (FR-015). It appears once: as a sheet on the next app open, or as the first screen of the review if the review is opened first. "Continue" marks them all as seen. Each task has a one-tap "Return to Next"; "Return all" returns every unreturned one. A returned task starts a new formulation (US2-4). The copy says what happened and that nothing was lost; it never says the person failed to act.

Not shown at all when nothing was parked. The screen reads local data, so it has no loading state on iOS; the web loading state is in D-03.
