M-16 · Review step: tasks that ask for a decision — iOS

The same decision card as M-03 and the same forms as M-04, laid out full-screen inside the review instead of as a sheet. One card at a time, oldest formulation first (US4-3, FR-034). The list is fixed when the step opens; a threshold change during the review does not reshuffle it (edge case). After each decision the next card slides in (brand easing, 200 ms; instant with Reduce Motion) with a one-line note of what just happened and an Undo that lasts about 5 seconds.
