M-03 · Decision card — iOS sheet (large detent)

Opened from the marker chip in M-01, from "Decide" in M-02, or one card at a time in the review (M-16). It is the same card in all three places (FR-010). One task, one decision. The optional reason sits above the decisions; picking one highlights a fitting decision without hiding or disabling any (FR-007). The decision list keeps the same order whatever is recommended, so the person's muscle memory is never broken.

Decided (owner, 2026-10-05): outside the review, the card is a large-detent sheet over Next. Every applied decision shows an Undo toast for about 5 seconds.

One-tap decisions: Done, Release to Someday, Cancel. Two-step decisions open a short form (M-04): Reformulate, Find a first step, Move to Waiting for, Keep 7 more days. The sheet scrolls when the content is taller than the screen; the decisions are in the lower, thumb-reachable half.

Reason → recommended decision: Unclear → Reformulate · Too big → Find a first step · Missing information → Find a first step · Waiting on someone → Move to Waiting for · Unpleasant / no energy → Find a first step · No longer matters → Cancel.
