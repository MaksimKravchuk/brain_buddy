Fresh start after update

027 FR-021, Constitution I and V. When the updated iPhone or Mac app first learns that its server runs the new release epoch, and earlier tasks, waiting changes or drafts exist on the device, it asks before deleting them. A new build alone never triggers this: against the old server the app works normally (.00). Flow: confirm → delete → existing sign-in → existing empty Inbox. iPhone on the left, Mac on the right. Buttons are illustrative.

.01 Confirm.03 Sign-in.04 Empty InboxOther states

M-02 / D-03 · .01 Confirm fresh start

Nothing is deleted until “Delete and continue”. “Not now” has initial focus and is the default; the destructive action is never default. Counts are shown when known.

.01 · Counts unknown

The tasks and waiting changes on this iPhone from the earlier version will be deleted. They can't be recovered.

Singular: “1 task”, “1 waiting change”. A zero part is dropped. If drafts exist, the list ends “and your drafts”.

.11 · After “Not now”

Your earlier data is untouched. Brain Buddy can't sync or sign in until you continue.

The same screen stays. No sync, no sign-in, no network. Asked again on next launch.

.02 · Deleting

Both buttons disabled; announced once after 1 s. No network.

M-02 / D-03 · .03 Signed out after deletion

The earlier session was deleted with the rest, so the existing sign-in sheet follows, unchanged. The new server starts empty, so the owner first creates the account with an invite, on the web or iPhone (invite step D-04 / M-03); the Mac cannot create accounts. Cancel leaves the empty account-less workspace.

M-02 / D-03 · .04 First-run empty Inbox

After sign-in (or Cancel) the existing empty Inbox appears. Existing copy, no new empty state.

M-02 / D-03 · Other states

.00 · Waiting for new server

New build; GET /api/meta returned 404 (old server, epoch 0) or hasn't answered (offline). The app works normally and syncs with the old server; nothing is asked or deleted. The check runs before any sign-in or sync push; server_epoch >= 1 moves to .01.

.05 · Couldn't delete

Brain Buddy couldn't finish deleting the earlier data.

Nothing was sent. Try again, or restart your iPhone and open Brain Buddy again.

Mac: “… restart your Mac …”. Sync and sign-in stay off until deletion finishes.

.06 · Interrupted

After confirmation: the next launch resumes the deletion without asking again. Before confirmation: nothing was deleted, and .01 is shown again.

.07 · Offline

At launch: the epoch is unknown, so the app stays in .00. After confirmation: deletion needs no network; the existing sign-in sheet shows its offline line (iPhone: “Your local tasks are kept. Connect to the internet to sign in.”, meaning tasks created since the fresh start; Mac: “Can't reach the server. Check your connection.”).

.08 · Fresh install or nothing to lose

The earlier store holds no tasks, waiting changes or drafts (or there is no earlier store): .01 is skipped; leftover files and the old session are removed without asking; then .03.
