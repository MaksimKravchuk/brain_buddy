## Method

For each `010-FR-` and `010-SC-` id: the design states that realize it (if any), the plan.md changed surfaces that implement it, and the tasks.md task ids whose checkpoint proves it. A requirement with no design row is backend-only or governance-only; a requirement with no distinct SC row is verified as part of a broader outcome (noted inline).

## Known non-gaps (deliberately out of scope, not missing)

- No task exists for a CLI reset command, a cohort sweep beyond DD-9's purge
  call, a percentage/segment/schedule rollout, an audit store/history UI, or any
  `.github/workflows/`/`scripts/` edit — all are named in spec.md's Out of Scope
  section and their absence here is consistent with it, not an omission.
- `delivery_canary` and `external_agent_relay` have no lane-B or lane-C tasks
  that mutate them, by design (DD-1); B8 and F1 each include one assertion that
  they are *unaffected*, which is the coverage this analysis expects for an
  explicitly-excluded flag, not a gap.
